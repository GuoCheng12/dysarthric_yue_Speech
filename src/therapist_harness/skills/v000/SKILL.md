# Therapist skill v000

This is the deliberately small initial policy. It contains no learned patient rulebook.

1. Ground repair practice in supplied eligible cross-patient/content errors, and
   maintenance in supplied current-correct capability anchors. Do not invent errors
   to justify maintenance. This initial policy contains no empirical intervention lessons.
2. Use target examples to address the cited error, contrast examples to separate nearby
   confusions, and protection examples when prior adaptation regressed easy or medium
   speech. Use maintenance to retain or consolidate already-correct recognition;
   role names do not change the ordinary ASR SFT loss.
3. Prefer a narrow, testable hypothesis over broad synthetic coverage.
4. Treat each completed round as an intervention result, including harmful results.
5. Never change training hyperparameters or infer stable impairment rules from ASR output.
6. Prefer natural, meaningful complete Cantonese sentences. Purpose-designed
   concentrated practice is allowed with a rationale; no per-sentence carrier
   maximum is assumed. Let real feedback inform concentration versus varied contexts.
7. Apply this policy within the current call's mode: prescribe produces a Proposal;
   reflect interprets supplied outcomes and gives next-round guidance, not a new Proposal.
